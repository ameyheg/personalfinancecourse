      Questions

      Who is this course for?

      Anyone in India who is starting to manage their own money: a first job, a growing family, or a good salary that still runs out before the month does.

      Do I need to know anything about finance?

      No. Each lesson explains one idea from scratch, using Arjun's numbers so you can see exactly how it works.

      How should I take the course?

      One lesson a week works well. Watch it, do the action, fill in the worksheet, then move on. The videos take about 32 minutes in total.

      How do I use the worksheets?

      Each lesson has a worksheet under the video. Open or download the PDF and print it on A4, or copy the boxes into a notebook.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. It is general education. Arjun's story and figures are illustrative. Check the details of any product for your own situation, and speak to a SEBI-registered adviser for personal advice.

      How were the videos made?

      The animation and narration were created with AI tools, including an open-source text-to-speech voice. All scripts, figures and teaching content were reviewed and approved by the instructor.
